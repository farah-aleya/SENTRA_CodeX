How SENTRA Works

From existing CCTV to a verified alert on the resident's phone

The Flow

  1. Existing CCTV streams video, no new cameras
  2. Edge AI: YOLOv8 finds people and vehicles, ByteTrack follows each one
  3. Rule engine checks zones, time and Away Mode
  4. Guard console gets the alert with a 10-second clip
  5. Guard confirms, then the resident is alerted on Telegram
  6. Every event is logged as evidence

Detection Rules

RuleFires when
Perimeter breachperson inside a restricted area
Away Mode breachanyone at a house whose owner is away
Loiteringperson stays at one house too long
Vehicle dwellvehicle stays in a zone too long
Camera healthcamera offline, frozen or covered

Time limits are shortened in this demo (real system: loitering 2 min, vehicle 30 min).

Privacy by Design

  • No facial recognition, no identity: only "person" or "vehicle"
  • Alerts judge where and how long, never appearance
  • Processed on site; only event clips are kept
  • A guard checks every alert before residents are told

What Is Real Here

  • Real SENTRA output: every box, zone, timer, alert, clip and snapshot was produced by the SENTRA code on GitHub
  • Replayed: camera feeds are recorded clips, played back with alerts at their original times
  • Simulated here: Confirm / Dismiss and the Telegram message run in your browser only. The working Telegram bot is shown in our video
