How it works

From camera to confirmed alert, with a human in charge

Every alarm passes two checks before anyone's phone buzzes.

STEP 1

Watch

OpenCV 5 runs YOLOv8 on your existing cameras and tracks people and vehicles in your zones.

STEP 2

Re-check

An AI agent on Amazon Bedrock re-examines the clip with OpenCV tools before deciding.

STEP 3

Guard decides

Real cases pop up for the guard with the clip and the agent's reasoning. One tap to approve.

STEP 4

Resident alerted

Only confirmed alerts reach the resident, with what happened and what to do next.

Features

Fewer false alarms. More trust.

Agentic triage

The agent calls OpenCV tools, looks again when the evidence is weak, and explains every decision step by step.

  • Contrast boost for night video
  • Optical-flow motion check
  • Full decision trace

Privacy by design

No facial recognition, ever. Faces and heads are blurred before any frame is saved, and only numbers go to the cloud.

  • OpenCV YuNet face blur
  • Clips only for allowed users
  • No images sent to the AI model

Away Mode

Going on holiday? Switch on Away Mode and any person at your house becomes a high-priority case.

  • One switch in the app
  • Choose 1 day to 1 week

Camera health

Know when a camera is covered, frozen or offline, before it matters.

  • Blocked lens detection
  • Frozen picture and offline alerts
